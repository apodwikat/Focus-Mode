Privacy Policy for Focus-Mode

Effective Date: September 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Focus-Mode ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension handles information when you use our service.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We do NOT collect, transmit, store, or share any personal user data.

All focus session timers, enabled/disabled states, and blocking rules are processed and stored exclusively on your local device using standard browser local storage (chrome.storage.local).

3. Browser Permissions

Our extension requests specific browser permissions solely to perform its core function of website blocking during active focus sessions:

  • declarativeNetRequest: Used locally to block access to specified distracting domain names during active focus sessions.
  • storage: Used to save your focus session preferences locally on your computer.
  • alarms: Used to run session timer countdowns locally.
  • tabs / activeTab: Used to detect tab state for active overlay displays.

4. Third-Party Services

Focus-Mode does not use third-party tracking, analytics, remote servers, or advertising networks.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact us via our official website at https://tawjihi.app.
